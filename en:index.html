Why I Do This

For more than twenty years, I have helped people buy property in Spain and in other countries. During all this time, I have seen the same pattern repeat itself, no matter where the buyer comes from or how much money they have. The problem is almost never a lack of resources. It is the lack of information at the right moment.

I have seen buyers with real wealth lose deposits because they did not understand what they were signing. I have seen financially solid profiles receive mortgage approvals for half of what they requested because nobody had explained how a bank evaluates repayment capacity. I have seen beautiful properties turn out to be non-financeable because they had a legal issue the seller did not know about.

Spain has its own logic. It is not difficult once you understand it. But it contains nuances—legal, banking, fiscal, documentary—that are not obvious from the outside.

This book and these services exist to help you understand those differences before they cost you time, money, or both.
