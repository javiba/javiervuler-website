Personalised Guidance

How Consultations Work

If you're considering a property purchase in Spain and need guidance tailored to your specific situation, consultations are available.

The approach is the same as the book's methodology: understanding your individual circumstances before making recommendations. This means discussing your financial situation, your objectives, your timeline, and the specific challenges you anticipate.

What to Expect

Consultations are structured conversations that provide clarity on the decisions you face. Rather than generic advice, guidance addresses your particular profile—whether you're a remote worker, an investor, a non-resident, or someone with specific financial circumstances that affect how Spanish banks will evaluate you.

The focus is on understanding the system and your position within it, so that you can make decisions with confidence.

Getting Started

To arrange a consultation, use the contact form to describe your situation and what you'd like to discuss. You'll receive a response outlining how the process works and next steps.
