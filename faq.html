Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this book for?

The book is written for international buyers considering property purchase in Spain: non-residents, expatriates, remote workers, investors, and anyone with income connected to another country. It addresses the specific challenges buyers from other countries face.

Do I need to read every chapter?

The book follows the typical property purchase journey, so chapters build on each other. However, you can focus on sections most relevant to your situation. The index and chapter summaries help you navigate.

Is the book updated when regulations change?

The book establishes principles and processes that remain stable. When significant regulatory changes occur, updates are published separately and linked from the website.

Can I get the book in different formats?

The book is available in English, Spanish, and French across digital platforms (Kindle, Apple Books, etc.) and paperback editions.

What's the difference between the book and the expertise articles?

The book provides the complete framework for understanding property purchase in Spain. The expertise articles address specific topics in greater depth or cover recent updates not in the original book.

How do consultations work?

Consultations are structured conversations tailored to your individual circumstances. Contact to describe your situation and what you'd like to discuss, and you'll receive information on how the process works.

Is there a glossary of Spanish property terms?

The book includes a practical glossary of Spanish property and legal terms you'll encounter in the purchase process.

How can I stay updated on property market changes?

Subscribe to the newsletter to receive occasional updates on regulatory changes, market shifts, and new articles published on this site.
