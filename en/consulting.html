The full process

Here's the real path, step by step, exactly as I explain it to every client before we start.

01

Starting point

You already have a property in mind, or want to know upfront how much the bank could lend you.

02

Profile review

I review your income, work history, type of employment, marital status, and tax filings to determine your borrowing capacity.

03

Choosing banks

I choose the banks that best match your profile and offer the best conditions. Their business managers give us a first read on whether it's feasible and what the terms would be.

04

Valuation

Once you agree to the terms and have signed the deposit, the property valuation takes place.

05

Risk department

With the valuation done, the file goes to the bank's Risk department, which gives final approval.

06

Binding offer and signing

The bank issues the FEIN and the FiAE, the binding offer. You choose a notary and, if buying with a mortgage, complete the legal transparency formalities before signing the deed.
