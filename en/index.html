The road to signing

The real process I work with, exactly as I explain it to every client.

01

Diagnosis

We start from your situation: whether you already have a property in mind or want to know upfront how much the bank could lend you, and we review income, work history, marital status, and tax filings to determine your borrowing capacity.

02

Banks and first validation

I choose the banks that best match your profile and offer the best conditions. Their business managers give us a first read on feasibility and terms.

03

Valuation and Risk

Once the deposit is signed, the valuation is carried out, and the file moves to the bank's Risk department, which gives final approval.

04

Offer and signing

The bank issues the FEIN and the FiAE (binding offer). You choose the notary, complete the legal transparency formalities, and we get to the deed together.
