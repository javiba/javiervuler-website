Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data controller

Javier Vulijscher (publishing as Javier Vuler), based at Pl. Sierra Ministra, 1, 28053 Madrid, Spain, is the data controller for the personal data collected through this website (javiervuler.com).

2. What data we collect

Through the forms on this site we collect only your email address, in two specific situations:

  • When you sign up to receive the free checklist.
  • When you redeem a promotional code to receive a copy of the book.

We do not collect any other personal data (we don't ask for phone number, address, or payment details) through these forms.

3. What we use your data for

  • To send you the content you requested (the checklist or the book).
  • If you subscribed to the newsletter, to occasionally send you content related to buying property in Spain and mortgage advisory.

We do not use your data for any other purpose, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Legal basis

Processing is based on your explicit consent, given by checking the corresponding box before submitting each form. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

5. Who we share your data with

We use the following providers to process your data:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, based in France, within the European Union), to manage email sending and the newsletter/checklist subscriber list.
  • Google (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script), to manage the free-book code redemption system.

Both providers act as data processors under their own privacy policies and comply with the GDPR.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your email for as long as you remain subscribed to our communications, or until you request its deletion. Code redemption data (email and date of use) is kept to prevent the same code from being reused.

7. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, and portability at any time by writing to hola@javiervuler.com or via WhatsApp at @javiervuler. You can also unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in every email.

If you believe your data is not being processed correctly, you also have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

8. Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your personal data against misuse and unauthorized access, limiting who can access it and reviewing these measures periodically.

9. Disclosure to third parties

We would only disclose your personal data if required by law, a court order, or a competent authority. If this business were ever sold or transferred in the future, your data may be passed on to the new owners, always under the same protection guarantees.

10. Third-party websites

This site may contain links to third-party websites (for example, Amazon or LinkedIn). This privacy policy does not apply to those websites; we recommend you read their own policies before using them.

11. Cookies

This site does not currently use tracking or analytics cookies of its own.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated in the future. Any relevant changes will be reflected on this same page, with the corresponding update date.
