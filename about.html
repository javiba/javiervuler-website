About Javier Vuler

Background

With over 20 years of experience in international real estate and mortgage finance, I have worked with buyers from more than 30 countries navigating property purchases across multiple markets.

My professional journey began in architecture and interior design. That foundation shaped how I view property: not simply as a financial asset, but as a place where people build their lives. Understanding the physical, financial, and personal dimensions of a property decision became central to my approach.

International Experience

I have lived and worked in Buenos Aires, New York, London, Paris, Bangkok, and Madrid. These years across different markets taught me how property systems work differently in each location, and how assumptions that hold true in one country often do not translate elsewhere.

That experience is why I eventually specialised in helping international buyers navigate Spain's property market. The transition from understanding property globally to focusing on one market specifically allows me to see both what is unique about Spain and what challenges international buyers face universally.

Expertise in Mortgage Finance

The shift into mortgage finance came from realising that most property problems for international buyers do not stem from finding the right property or negotiating the price. They stem from not understanding how financing works in the target country.

Spanish banks evaluate borrowers differently than banks in other countries. Currency risk, income documentation, residency status, and professional background all carry different weight. A buyer who understands these factors before applying for a mortgage makes fundamentally better decisions.

Approach

My core principle is straightforward: better decisions begin with better understanding. This is why I write, why I consult, and why this book exists.

I do not approach property purchases as transactions to optimise for speed or lowest cost. I approach them as financial and personal decisions that shape someone's life for years to come. That distinction changes everything about how the process should unfold.
